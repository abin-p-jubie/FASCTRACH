Assessment & Assignment Management

⚠️ Academic Integrity & AI Usage Guidelines

  • All submitted assignments and assessments must be your own original work.
  • Use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Grammarly, etc.) is allowed only for guidance — not for full content generation.
  • Proper citations must be provided for any external references.
  • Late submissions may result in penalties unless approved by your department.
  • Always review the instructions provided by your instructor before submission.
